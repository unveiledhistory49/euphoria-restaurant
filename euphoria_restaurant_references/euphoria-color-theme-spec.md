### 2. Theme Direction

**Mood**  
Luxurious • Sensual • Nightlife-elegant with a romantic tropical-garden edge.

**Core Aesthetic**  
Dark-mode leaning foundation (Deep Black + Emerald Teal) elevated by the signature soft rose-gold / pink identity. Warm Soft Brass details and occasional Burgundy Red accents create premium hierarchy. The overall feeling should echo the logo and printed menus: refined, glowing, and intimate rather than harsh nightclub black-and-gold.

**Visual Principles**
- High contrast between dark surfaces and luminous pink/rose-gold elements.
- Soft pink typography on all dark backgrounds (matching the physical menus).
- Gold reserved exclusively for thin lines, underlines, icons, and “premium” moments.
- Botanical softness introduced via blush pink and lavender (inspired by the cherry-blossom tree).
- Clean, spacious layout with generous white/ivory breathing room in content sections.

---

### 3. Application Guidelines

**Backgrounds**
- Primary: Deep Black (`#0A0A0A`)
- Secondary: Emerald Teal (`#1A6B5F`) for alternating sections
- Light content areas: Soft Ivory (`#F9F6F2`)

**Typography Colour**
- Headings on dark: Euphoria Rose Gold or Soft Pink
- Body text on dark: Soft Pink (`#F5C6D0`) or White
- Body text on light: Deep Black

**Interactive Elements**
- Primary buttons: Euphoria Rose Gold background + Deep Black text
- Secondary buttons: Emerald Teal background + Soft Pink / White text
- Hover / Active states: slight brightness increase or Soft Brass underline
- Links: Euphoria Rose Gold, Soft Brass underline on hover

**Decorative Elements**
- Horizontal rules / dividers: 1 px Soft Brass
- Icons: Soft Brass or Soft Pink
- Cards / panels: Soft Ivory with subtle Soft Brass border or deep shadow on black

**Photography Treatment**
- Food and interior imagery should retain natural colour; do not force brand tints.
- Overlays on hero images: Deep Black at 40–60% opacity to ensure text legibility.

---

### 4. Do’s and Don’ts

**Do**
- Maintain the soft metallic quality of the rose-gold wherever possible.
- Use Soft Pink for all body text on dark surfaces (exact menu match).
- Keep gold usage minimal and elegant.

**Don’t**
- Introduce pure neon pinks or saturated hot pinks.
- Use pure nightclub gold/black without the rose-gold and teal balance.
- Apply bright chilli red or heavy caramel tones outside of food photography.

---

**Summary Statement**  
The website must feel like the Euphoria logo and menu cards brought to life: elegant black and deep-green foundations, a soft glowing rose-gold/pink identity, warm brass details, and restrained burgundy accents — luxurious, sensual, and botanically refined.
